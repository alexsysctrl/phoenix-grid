The Project consists of two new collection substations (Project Substations) and
two single-circuit 500 kV tie lines (Tie Lines) in La Paz County, on land owned by La
Paz County, that will deliver electricity generated at the Atlas Solar Project to the
regional grid at Cielo Azul Switchyard, approved by the Commission in Decision No.
78302.
Two CECs are being approved for the Project to allow for future ownership
transfer of a portion of the Project. This CEC, CEC-2, is for one of die two Project
Substations and one of the Tie Lines. The Companion CEC-1 is for the other Project
Substation and Tie Line. The Project route commences at the 25-acre Project Substation
Siting Area (see Exhibit 1). From the new substation, the Project heads north to the
adjacent Cielo Azul Switchyard, where the Project will interconnect to the regional grid.
The Project Substation will occupy an area of approximately six acres in the approved
Substation Siting Area located within the Solar Project boundary as shown in Exhibit l.
The Tie Line will be located within the Substation Siting Area and the certificated Cielo
Azul Switchyard as shown in Exhibit 1. The typical transmission structure heights will
not exceed 199 feet.
