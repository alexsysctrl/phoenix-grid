A. INTRODUCTION.
Pursuant to notice given as provided by law, the Arizona Power Plant and
Transmission Line Siting Committee (Committee) held a public hearing on March 14
through March 15, 2022 in Florence, Arizona, in conformance with the requirements of
Arizona Revised Statutes (A.R.S.) §§ 40.360, et seq., for the purpose of receiving
evidence and deliberating on the application (Application) of BOCA bn, LLC (Applicant)
for a Certificate of Environmental Compatibility (Certificate) in the above-captioned
case.

B. P RO J ECT O VERVIEW.
The Project consists of a new collection substation (Project Substation) located in
Pinal County and a single-circuit 230 kV gen-tie (Gen-Tie) that will deliver electricity
generated at the Box Canyon Solar Project to the regional grid at the existing Salt River
Project Abel Substation located in the Town of Florence.
The Project route commences at the Project Substation (see Exhibit 1). From the
new substation, the Project heads due west on State Trust land and then proceeds
southwest along the Magma Arizona Railroad right-of-way to connect to the existing
