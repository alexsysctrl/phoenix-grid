Pursuant to notice given as provided by law, the Arizona Power Plant and
Transmission Line Siting Committee ("Committee") held public hearings on December 9
through December l l, 2024, in Flagstaff, Coconino County, Arizona, in conformance with
the requirements of the Arizona Revised Statutes ("A.R.S.") §40-360 et seq. for the purpose
of receiving evidence and deliberating on the October 25, 2024 Application of Snowflake
Solar, LLC ("Applicant") for Certificates of Environmental Compatibility ("Certificates"
or "CEC") authorizing construction of a new 500 kilovolt ("kV") transmission line within
portions of the City of Holbrook and portions of unincorporated Navajo County, Arizona
(the "Project").

B . P RO J E CT DE SCRIP TIGN
The Project consists of building a new single circuit 500 kV alternating current
generation intertie transmission line (the "Gen-Tie") up to 18 miles long. The Project will
connect the planned Snowflake Solar Energy Facility ("Solar Facility"), a 1,000 MW solar
photovoltaic generating facility with a 1,000 MW battery energy storage system, from the
non-jurisdictional Solar Facility substation ("Solar Facility Substation") to the existing
Decision No. 79718


=== PAGE 6 ===
APS Cholla Substation. The Project will be located on private property, trust lands
administered by Arizona State Land Department ("ASLD"), City of Holbrook property,
and Arizona Department of Transportation ("ADOT") right-of-way ("ROW"). The Solar
Facility and Solar Facility Substation will be located on private lands entirely within
unincorporated Navajo County.
Two Certificates are being approved for the Project to allow for the future transfer
of ownership of a portion of the Project to APS. The Applicant will own the portion of the
Gen-Tie from the Solar Facility Substation to the point of change of ownership ("POCO")
("CEC-240A"), and APS will own the portion of the Gen-Tie from the POCO to the point
of interconnection at the Cholla Substation ("CEC-240B"). The portion of the Project
covered by CEC-240A will be constructed, owned, and operated by the Applicant. The
portion of the Project covered by CEC-240B will be constructed, owned, and operated by
APS.
Route and Corridor
CEC-240A: The Gen-Tie route for CEC-240A will begin at the Solar Facility
Substation approximately 2.5 miles northwest of Interstate 40 ("I-40") and approximately
6.5 miles north of downtown Holbrook. From the Solar Facility Substation, the Gen-Tie
proceeds southwest approximately 2.9 miles, then south approximately 2.0 miles, then
southwest approximately 2.5 miles, then west approximately 1.6 miles before turning
southerly to cross 1-40. The 1-40 crossing shall be immediately west of exit 283, Perkins
Valley Road, and shall be approximately 0.2 mile in length. After crossing 1-40, the Gen-
Tie proceeds west approximately 2.3 miles, then turns northwest approximately 1.6 miles
to the POCO with APS, as shown on Exh ibit A. The portion of the route covered by CEC-
240A is approximately 14 miles long.
The corridor for CEC-240A begins at the Solar Facility Substation and will end at
the eastern edge of the existing APS Cholla-Snowflake 69kV line. The proposed width of
the corridor for CEC-240A is between 300 feet to 1,500 feet, as shown on Exh ibit A.

C E C -240B: The route of the westernmost segment of the Project from the POC()
to the point of interconnection at the Cholla Substation covered by CEC-240B is planned
to run south of Cholla Lake and north of the existing Burlington Northern Santa Fe
Railway, as shown on Exhibit A. The total length of the portion of the route covered by
CEC-240B is approximately 4 miles.
The corridor width is variable throughout this area as it generally covers the APS-
owned land with a maximum width of 4,640 feet and a minimum width of 840 feet. The
corridor for CEC-240B starts at approximately 0.5 miles east of the existing APS Cholla-
Snowflake 69kV line and as it proceeds west the corridor widens to encompass the Cholla
Power Plant property, which includes both the norther and southern sides of Cholla Lake,
with the southern boundary of the corridor being the B NS F railroad and 1-40 as the northern
boundary as shown on Exhibit A.
The total length of the Project shall be up to 18 miles. The Applicant requests
approval of an approximately 300-foot-wide ROW within the corridor. The Applicant
anticipates constructing four to nine structures per mile (with an estimated total of 85
structures, depending upon final engineering and design) spaced approximately 300 to
1,900 feet apart with a height not exceeding 199 feet, all subject to final design and
engineering.

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OF LAW
This Certificate incorporates the following Findings of Fact and Conclusions of
Law:
l. The Project aids the State and the Southwest Region of the United States in
meeting the need for an adequate, economical, and reliable supply of electric power.
2. When constructed in compliance with the conditions imposed in this
D e c is io n  N o . 7 9 7 1 8

Certificate, the Project aids the State, preserving a safe and reliable electric transmission
system.
3. During the course of the hearing, the Committee considered evidence on the
environmental compatibility of the Project as required by A.RS. §40-360 el seq.
4. The Project and the conditions placed on the Project in this Certificate
effectively minimize the impact of the Project on the environment and ecology of the State.
5. The conditions placed on the Project in this Certificate resolve matters
concerning balancing the need for the Project with its impact on the environment and
ecology of the State arising during the course of the proceedings and, as such, serve as
findings and conclusions on such matters.
6. The Project is in the public interest because the Project's contribution to
meeting the need for an adequate, economical, and reliable supply of electric power
outweighs the minimized impact of the Project on the environment and ecology of the
State.
7. The Project substations are not jurisdictional because the definition of a
"transmission line" under A.R.S. § 40-360(l0) only includes "new switchyards to be used
therewith," not substations.
