below.
B . PROJECT OVERVIEW.
The Project consists of the construction and operation of three separate double-
circuit capable 230kV transmission lines needed to serve a new data center and support
overall growth in the west valley. The three separate 230kV transmission line routes
will connect existing 230kV transmission lines and substations to the recently
constructed Contrail Substation.
