The Project consists of a 2.0 to 3.0-mile 500 kV transmission line and associated
interconnection facilities to interconnect the Applicant's non-jurisdictional Atlas North
Solar Project (the "Generating Facility") to the regional electrical transmission grid at the
Cielo Azul Switchyard in La Paz County.
All Project facilities will be located in a siting area as shown in (the
"Siting Area"). The Project will originate at the non-jurisdictional Project Substation
proposed to be located within the boundaries of the Generating Facility. From the boundary
of the Generating Facility, the Project will be routed south in a six-hundred-foot corridor
for approximately 0.4 mile until it reaches the point of interconnection, the Cielo Azul
Switchyard.
The final right-of-way width for the Project within the Siting Area will be 200 feet.
The maximum height of the structures will be 199 feet.
