17 Pursuant to A.R.S. § 40-360, et seq., after due consideration of all relevant matters, the

19 Environmental Compatibility ("CEC-2") issued by the Arizona Power Plant and Transmission Line

22 public interest, including the need for an adequate, economical, and reliable supply of electric power

24 that granting the Project a CEC-2 is in the public interest.

11 its successors and assigns, this Certificate and a companion Certificate for construction of the
12 Randolph 230kV Gen-Tie Project ("Project") as described below.
13
14 The Project will consist of one (1) 230kV transmission line and one (1) new
15 substation to interconnect the Applicant's planned Randolph Solar Park (a 200 MW solar
16 photovoltaic power plant that may be paired wide a battery energy storage system) to the
regional transmission system at the existing SRP Randolph Substation. Two CECs are being
17 approved for the Project to allow for future ownership transfer of a portion of the Project.
18 This CEC, CEC-2, is for the portion of the Project that originates at the point of future
19 ownership change (the Point of Change in Ownership ("PCO") near the Randolph Substation
20 property boundary and the point of interconnection ("POI") at the Randolph Substation. The
21 companion CEC-l is for the portion of the Project from and including the Project Substation
22 as defined in CEC-l to the PCO.
23 The Project route for CEC-2 commences at the PCO. From the PCO, the route
24 continues south approximately 0.18-mile, until it is parallel to the Randolph Substation
25 switchyard. The route then travels approximately 0.04-mile eastward to the POI. The overall
26 length of the Project approved in this CEC-2 is approximately .22 miles from the PCO.
