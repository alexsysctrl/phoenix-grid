B . P RO J ECT O VERVIEW.
The Project consists of a new collector substation (Project Substation) located in
Maricopa County and a single-circuit 500 kV gen-tie (Gen-Tie) that will transmit
electricity generated at the Applicant's hybrid solar photovoltaic and battery power plant
(the Generating Facility) to the regional grid at the existing Delaney Substation
approximately 3.5 miles northeast of the Generating Facility.

Applicant has requested approval of a preferred alignment and two alternative
alignments for the Gen-Tie, as shown on Exhibit 1. Alternative A is Applicant's
preferred alignment, and it runs due east from the Project Substation along Courthouse
Road to 475"' Avenue, where it then angles diagonally northeast, crossing the Salome
Highway and entering the Delaney Substation from the south, a total distance of
approximately 3.5 miles. Alternative B and Alternative C would both run due north from
the Project Substation along 483"' Avenue to Thomas Road, and then run due east along
opposite sides of Thomas Road, crossing the Salome Highway, and entering the Delaney
Substation from the south (Alternative B) and the west (Alternative C), a distance of
approximately 4.5 miles. Applicant is requesting the two alternative alignments because
the preferred Alternative A alignment crosses U.S. Bureau of Land Management land and
Applicant's application for a right-of-way permit is still pending.
The Project Substation will occupy an area of approximately four acres in the
approved Substation Siting Area located within the Generating Facility boundary as
shown on Exhibit 1. The Gen-Tie line will require a Right-of-Way (ROW) of up to 200
feet within a corridor ranging from 500 feet to up to 1,000 feet wide in specific locations
as shown on Exhibit l. The typical transmission structure heights will be in the range of
115 to 135 feet and will not exceed 140 feet.
