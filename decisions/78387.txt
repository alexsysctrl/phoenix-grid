Legal Description of Substation and Gen-Tie Corridor
The corridor for the CEC ranges in width from 450-feet along the gen-tie to 650-feet at the Project
Substation and provides a near direct path from the Project Substation to the existing Halo
Substation. The corridor begins within the Storey Solar generation facility located in the
southwest quarter of Section 22 Township 6S Range 8E Gila & Salt River Baseline & Meridian
(G&SRB&M) at 32 52' 50" N latitude and 111 30'35" S longitude. At this location, the corridor
extends 650-foot wide for approximately 650 feet south to accommodate the Project Substation.
From there, the corridor width is reduced to 450 feet and proceeds south for approximately0.34
mile, parallel to the east of the existing Pinal Central to Randolph and Pinal Central to Browning
transmission lines to a point within the northwest quarter of Section 27 Township 6S Range 8E
G&SRB&M at 32 52'30 N latitude and 111 30'35" S longitude. From that location, the 450-foot
wide corridor proceeds west for approximately 0.10 mile concluding at a point within the
northwest quarter of Section 27 Township 6S Range 8E G&SRB&M at 32 52'30" latitude and 111
30' 41". The corridor is primarily on privately owned land with the exception of a portion crossing
over the City of Coolidge owned Steele Road and Union Pacific owned railroad. A map of the
Project showing the described corridor is included as part of this Exhibit 1.
