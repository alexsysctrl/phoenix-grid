18 The Project consists of an approximately5-mile 500-kV transmission line and

20 facilities (the non-jurisdictional "Wind Project") to the regional electrical transmission
21 grid at a planned APS Switchyard ("APS Switchyard") in Coconino County on the
22 Moenkopi-Cedar Mountain 500-kV transmission line.

24 "Project Corridor"). The requested Project Corridor extends between the non-
25 jurisdictional Project Substation and the APS Switchyard. The Project Corridor starts as a
26 rectangular area buffered around the planned location for the Project Substation on private
27 property in Township 26N, Section 6E, Range 7. The Project Corridor then proceeds
