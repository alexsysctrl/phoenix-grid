Pursuant to A.R.S. § 40-360, et seq., after due consideration of all relevant matters, the
Arizona Corporation Commission ("Commission") finds and concludes that the Certificate of
Environmental Compatibility ("CEC") issued by the Arizona Power Plant and Transmission Line
Siting Committee ("Siting Committee") is hereby approved as granted by this Order.
The Commission, in reaching its decision, has balanced all relevant matters in the broad
public interest, including the need for an adequate, economical, and reliable supply of electric power
with the desire to minimize the effect thereof on the environment and ecology of this state, and finds
that granting the Project a CEC is in the public interest.

B. PROJ ECT DESCRIPTION
The Project will consist of a 230kV generation tie line and the associated Project
Substation needed to interconnect the Applicant's planned 300MW solar photovoltaic
generating facility that will be paired with a battery energy storage system ("Solar
Project") to the regional transmission system at the existing Pinal Central Substation. The
Project consists of underground and above ground segments. Only the above ground
segments are covered by the Application and considered by the Committee.
Two CECs are being approved for the Project to allow for the transfer of the
portion of the Project covered by CEC-2 to Salt River Agricultural Improvement and
Power District ("SRP"). This CEC, CEC-1, is for the portion of the Project Hom and
including the Project Substation to the future point of change of ownership (PCO). The
companion CEC, CEC-2, is for the portion of the Project that originates at the PCO near
