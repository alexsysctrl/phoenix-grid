The Project consists of a new collection substation (Project Substation) located in Pima
County and/or Pinal County and a double-circuit gen-tie (Gen-Tie) that will deliver electricity
generated at the Serrano Solar and Storage Project to the regional grid as the existing Arizona
Public Service (APS) Saguaro Facility and/or the existing Tucson Electric Power (TEP) Tortolita
Substation. The first phase of the Project (Phase 1) is anticipated to interconnect at the APS
Saguaro Facility at 230 kilovolts (kV). Subsequent phases (Phase 2) may interconnect at the APS
Saguaro Facility at 230 kV or l 15 kv, or at the TEP Tortolita Substation at 138 kv.
Two CECs are being approved for the Project to allow for future ownership transfer of a
portion of the Project. This CEC, CEC-l, is for the portion of the Project that originates at the
Project Substation to the Point of Change of Ownership (the POCO) near the Saguaro Substation

The Project route for CEC-1 commences at the Project Substation (at Nl, see Exhibit 1).
From the new substation, the Project parallels Pinal Airpark Road and then Interstate 10 (I-10)
for approximately five miles in Pinal County, Arizona, before crossing 1-10 to the POCO
(Exhibit l, N4). CEC-1 also include the Gen-Tie segment tointerconnect Phase 2 into the TEP

Project Substation will occupy an area of approximately 10 acres in the approved Substation
Siting Area located within the Solar Project boundary as shown in Exhibit 1. The Gen-Tie line
will require a 150-foot Right-of-Way (ROW) within the 1,000-foot routing corridor evaluated in12
