Two CECs are being approved for the Project to allow for the potential assignment
of the portion of the Project covered by CEC-2 to APS. This CEC, CEC-1, is for the

Project that originates at the POGO near the Project Substation to the point of
interconnection at the Orchard Substation.
Project facilities covered by CEC-1 include: (1) the Project Substation to step up
power from the Orchard Solar Facility to transmission voltage, (2) the 230 kV
transmission line segment from the green hydrogen facility to the Project Substation, and
(3) the green hydrogen facility's up to eight electrolyze substations, each of which
includes a 230 kV to 34.5 kV transformer to step down power from transmission voltage
to serve the green hydrogen facility.
All Project facilities covered by CEC-1 are within the Facility Siting Area, which is
a rectangular area extending 2,600 feet east from South Avenue IE and 1,250 feet south
from East County 19th Street, as shown in Exhibit A.
