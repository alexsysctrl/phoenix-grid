The Applicant previously obtained a Certificate of Environmental Compatibility in
Decision 78590 (Docket L-21185A-22-0072-00201). This Application is in addition to that
Certificate of Environmental Compatibility and includes the information and studies required
pursuant to A.R.S §40-360.01 et seq. and Arizona Administrative Code R14-3-201 ez seq.
As described more fully in the Application and shown on the attached Exhibit A, the
Project would include a single 500-kV generation transmission line extending the previously
approved HV Sunrise gen-tie (see Decision 78590) approximately 8 miles southwest. The
Project would also include an approximately 5~mile-long gen-tie which would originate from
the collector substation at the Harquahala Sun I generating facility and extend northeast to the
Delaney Substation, parallel to the previously approved HV Sunrise gen-tie. Ultimately, side-
by-side 500-kV generation transmission lines, within a single corridor, will extend
approximately 5 miles in length from near the intersection of Courthouse Road and 495th
Avenue northeast to the Delaney Substation. The typical transmission structure heights willbe
in the range of 115 to 165 feet and will not exceed 199 feet. The Applicant is requesting a 200
foot wide right-of-way for each 500 kV transmission line and a condor of between 500 and
6,500 feet wide as depicted on Exhibit A.
As further depicted on the map attached as Exhibit A, the Project would include up to
three collector substations approximately 6 to 25 acres in size each. The collector substation
for Harquahala Sun I is anticipated to be located near the southeast comer of the intersection of
West Courthouse Road and 495th Avenue, approximately five miles west of the existing
Delaney Substation. The collector substations for Harquahala Sun II are anticipated to be
located (1) near the northwest comer of the intersection of Broadway Road and 495th Avenue,
approximately seven miles southwest of the existing Delaney substation, and (2) near the
