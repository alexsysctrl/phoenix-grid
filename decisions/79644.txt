B. PROJECT DESCRIPTION
The Project consists of an approximately 2.0-mile 230 kV transmission line and
associated interconnection facilities to interconnect the Applicant's non-jurisdictional Eagle
Eye Solar Project to the regional electrical transmission grid at the Harcuvar Substation in
La Paz County.
All Project facilities will be located in a variable-width corridor as shown in Exhibit
A (the "Project Corridor"). The Project will originate at the non-jurisdictional Project
Substation proposed to be located in Section 28, Township 7 North, Range 12 West. From
the Project Substation, the Project will be routed north-northwest for approximately 1.2
miles, then turn northwest for approximately 0.8 miles, paralleling the Western Area Power
Administration ("WAPA")-operated Liberty to Parker 230-kV transmission lines, until it
reaches the point of interconnection, the Harcuvar Substation. If required by WAPA, the
Project will alternatively interconnect at a new switchyard in the Project Corridor adjacent
