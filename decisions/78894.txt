B. P RO J E CT DE SCRIP TIO N
The Project will consist of a double-circuit capable 230kV transmission line and
associated interconnection facilities to interconnect the Applicant's planned solar energy
generating facility of up to 300 MW (the "Orchard Solar l*acility") and a green hydrogen
production and liquefaction facility to the regional electrical transmission grid at the
existing Arizona Public Service Company ("APS") Orchard Substation. The Project may
consist of both underground and above ground segments. Only the above ground
segments are covered by the Application and considered by the Committee. The first
circuit is needed to serve the Project, the second circuit is needed to serve future growth in
the area.

Two CECs are being approved for the Project to allow for the potential assignment
of the portion of the Project covered by CEC-2 to APS. This CEC, CEC-2, is for the
portion of the Project from the future point of change of ownership ("POCO") near the
Project Substation to the point of interconnection at the Orchard Substation. The
companion CEC, CEC-1, is for the portion of the Project from and including the facilities
located within the Facility Siting Area to the POCO.
All Project facilities covered by CEC-2 will be within a 120-foot right of way, to
be located in the corridor shown in Exhibit A. The CEC-2 corridor begins at the
intersection of County l9th Street and Avenue 1E and proceeds east for 1.5 miles, this
portion of the corridor is 500 feet wide, centered on East County I 9th Street. Starting at
the right-of-way for Avenue 2 % E and extending approximately 1,000 feet east, the Gen-
Tie corridor is 250 feet and only occupies land north of County 19th Street. After that
point, the corridor continues east to State Route ("SR") 195 as a 500-foot-wide area
centered on County 19th Street. At SR 195, the corridor turns north and proceeds to a
point before East County 18 % Street, this portion of the corridor width gradually widens
from 500 feet to about 750 feet, with SR 195 serving as the corridor's easter boundary.
North of County 18 % Street, the corridor occupies land on the Barry M. Goldwater Range
("BMGR"), west of SR 195 and south of County 14th Street. Once the corridor is on the
BMGR, its width varies from approximately 450 feet to approximately 7,700 feet.

FINDINGS OF FACT AND CONCLUSIONS OFLAW
This Certificate incorporates the following Findings of Fact and Conclusions of
L a w:
l . The Project aids the state and the southwest region of the United States in
meeting the need for an adequate, economical, and reliable supply of renewable electric
power.
2. The Project aids the state, preserving a safe and reliable electric
transmission system.
3. During the course of the hearing, the Committee considered evidence on the
environmental compatibility of the Project as required by A.RS. §40-360 et seq.
4. The Project and the conditions placed on the Project in this Certificate
effectively minimize the impact of the Project on the environment and ecology of the
state.
5. The conditions placed on the Project in this Certificate resolve matters
concerning balancing the need for the Project with its impact on the environment and
ecology of the state arising during the course of the proceedings, and, as such, serve as
findings and conclusions on such matters.
6. The Project is in the public interest because the Project's contribution to
meeting the need for an adequate, economical, and reliable supply of electric power
outweighs the minimized impact of the Project on the environment and ecology of the
state.
