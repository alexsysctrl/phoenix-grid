On December 13, 2022. Randolph Solar Park, LLC ("Randolph" or "Applicant")
submitted an Application ("Application") requesting the Arizona Corporation Commission
("Commission") amend Decision Nos. 78588 and 78589, which approved two Certificates of
Environmental Compatibility ("CEC") in Line Siting Case 200 (collectively "CEC-I" and "CEC-
2") that authorized the Randolph 230 kilovolt ("kV") Gen-Tie Project ("Project"). The Project
consists of a new 230kV transmission line and substation that would connect with the existing Salt
River Project Agricultural and Power District ("SRP") 230kV Randolph Switchyard.

The Application requests the Commission amend CEC-I and CEC-° to remedy
minor errors regarding the structures to be built by the Applicant and its assignees. Specifically. the
original application contains a typographical error stating that two monopoles will be constructed
within the corridor approved in CEC-". but it should have reflected that three poles will be built
instead. In addition. the original application also states that the monopoles in the CEC-l corridor
would be constructed of weathered galvanized steel material with a patina finish. However. the
Applicant intends to use the same galvanized steel materials in both CEC corridors. Finally. the
Applicant originally indicated that the monopoles would range in height from 85 to 10() feet. Instead.
the Applicant intents to construct poles between 80 to 100 feet in height.
